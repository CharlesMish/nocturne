## Fast evaluation checklist

- No voices.
- No music.
- No sudden loud transients.
- No obvious birds if it is meant for sleep/night.
- Low traffic/mechanical noise unless that is the point.
- At least 30 seconds, preferably 60–180 seconds.
- Loops acceptably after a small crossfade.
- License shown as CC0 on the source page at download time.
